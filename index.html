01 / THE RESEARCH CONTEXT

Research Overview

Fashion retail decisions often span disconnected inventory, marketing, fitting, and customer-behaviour processes. This research explores a shared AI architecture that brings those perspectives together to support more informed decisions.

01

Intelligent Retail Operations

Explore demand and stock movement together to support coordinated inventory decisions across retail locations.

02

Personalised Customer Experiences

Connect marketing, sizing, and styling intelligence to better understand individual fashion retail needs.

03

Unified Data & AI Architecture

Bring four research functions into one system through shared application services and a common data layer.
