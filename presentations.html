02 / A RESEARCH DIRECTION THAT DEVELOPED

The Evolution of Our Research

From the early proposal to the final paper, the project refined its methods while keeping the goal of one connected research system.

  1. 01

    Initial Proposal

    An integrated concept linking CCTV emotion/behaviour sensing, smart mirror recommendations, forecasting, and marketing.

  2. 02

    PP1 Refinement

    Panel feedback reshaped the scope: GPS replaced CCTV emotion detection, fitting became more practical, and inventory and marketing contributions were clarified.

  3. 03

    PP2 Development

    Updated workflows connect component inputs, model outputs, retail decisions, and shared data. Further enhancements and real-world validation remain planned work.

  4. 04

    Final Integrated Research

    The paper documents four functions linked through common FastAPI services and Firestore, with model evaluation, limitations, and future research.

Historical stages and source limitations

Research-stage summaries are based on the proposal, PP1, PP2, and final paper, not on filenames or assumed assessment outcomes. No dates, grades or completion percentages are assigned.

PP1 records explicit prototype feedback. PP2 provides workflows and future directions, but no separate verified user-feedback account. Earlier marketing and virtual-fitting technology choices remain part of the original decks; final methods are described in the research paper and Domain page.
