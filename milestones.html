Research Milestones

See how the research evolved ↓

The timeline combines the confirmed assessment schedule with the separately archived research paper. Supporting file availability does not establish an assessment result.

  1. 01
    RESEARCH INITIATIONConducted

    Proposal Presentation

    16 March 2026

    The initial proposal introduced an integrated AI retail concept linking customer experience, stock flow, and personalised marketing.

    Key research activities

    • CCTV-based emotion and behaviour detection, smart mirror recommendations, inventory forecasting, and personalised marketing were proposed together.
    • Early objectives and architecture directions connected sensing, recommendations, forecasting, and campaign decisions.
    • These were historical proposals, not a description of the final implemented technology.

  2. 02
    SCOPE REFINEMENT & PROTOTYPINGConducted

    Progress Presentation 1 (PP1)

    11 May 2026

    PP1 records how panel feedback refined the research problems and the proposed solutions, alongside component prototypes and reported user feedback.

    Key research activities

    • CCTV emotion detection was replaced with GPS-based behaviour tracking; complex VR/AR was replaced with a 2D API-based virtual try-on direction.
    • Inventory work was reframed around forecasting, stock optimisation, and decision support; marketing became more promotion-focused.
    • Prototype feedback included indoor GPS accuracy, clearer alerts, try-on image quality and speed, and additional inventory analytics.

02 / FEEDBACK INTO RESEARCH DESIGN

How Our Research Evolved

The PP1 “Proposal Feedback & Improvements” slide records the following changes. They preserve the early research direction while explaining the later refinement.

  1. 01

    Initial Direction

    CCTV emotion detection, smart mirror recommendations, inventory forecasting and marketing intelligence formed the early integrated concept.

  2. 02

    Panel Feedback

    PP1 records concerns about real-time sensing complexity, VR/AR expertise, inventory justification and the marketing contribution.

  3. 03

    Refined Research Direction

    GPS tracking, practical API-based virtual fitting, forecast-driven inventory decisions and a clearer promotion-focused pipeline.

Initial Direction → Panel Feedback → Refined Research Direction (PP1, slide 2)
Research functionInitial DirectionPanel FeedbackRefined Research Direction
Customer assistanceCCTV emotion detectionReal-time CCTV emotion detection was challenging.GPS-based movement, zone and dwell-time tracking for staff assistance.
Virtual fittingComplex smart mirror / VR-AR approachAdvanced VR/AR expertise was a concern.A practical 2D API-based virtual try-on direction with size prediction.
InventoryInventory forecasting and optimisationThe problem justification needed to distinguish the work from existing inventory systems.AI forecasting, stock optimisation and real-time decision support.
MarketingMarketing intelligence dashboardThe promotion link and research contribution needed clarification.Promotion-focused prediction, performance analysis and generated posters.

PP1 describes an earlier marketing prototype using Random Forest and Stable Diffusion. PP2's marketing workflow figure shows RFM and XGBoost redemption prediction. The final paper remains the authority for the final methods and evaluation.

PP1 contains explicit prototype-feedback sections. PP2 documents updated workflows and future work but does not provide a separate user-feedback account or a verified full-platform completion outcome. Feedback is attributed to PP1 rather than invented for PP2.

PP2's virtual-fitting text mentions Gemini, while its workflow figure describes Vertex AI. The final paper identifies Vertex AI virtual try-on; the historical slides remain unmodified.
