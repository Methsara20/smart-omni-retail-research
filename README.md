# AI-Driven Smart Fashion Retail Recommendation System

- **Institution:** Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology (SLIIT)

## Project Overview

The Smart Omni-Retail System is an AI-driven research project designed to enhance fashion retail operations and customer experiences through intelligent inventory management, personalised marketing, virtual try-on technology, and customer behaviour analysis.

The research integrates four intelligent components into a unified retail platform to support data-driven decisions, improve operational efficiency, and deliver more personalised shopping experiences.

## Research Components

### 1. Smart Inventory & Stock Flow Optimisation

Uses machine learning for demand forecasting, inventory monitoring, stock replenishment recommendations, and inter-store stock movement optimisation.

### 2. Personalised Marketing Intelligence

Applies customer analytics and machine learning to identify relevant promotional opportunities and support personalised marketing decisions.

### 3. AI-Based Virtual Try-On & Size Recommendation

Combines AI-powered virtual garment visualisation with machine learning-based clothing size recommendations to improve the online shopping experience.

### 4. GPS-Based Customer Behaviour Tracking & Staff Assistance

Analyses customer movement and shopping behaviour to support intelligent staff assistance and improve in-store customer service.
