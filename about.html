03 / CONNECTED RESEARCH

Four Research Areas. One Intelligent Platform.

These contributions form one research platform. The current system architecture uses a shared Flutter interface, FastAPI services and MongoDB data that connect the four functions.

01

Inventory intelligence

Demand forecasting, stock flow and explainable retail decisions.

02

Marketing intelligence

Customer analytics, promotion prediction and personalised content.

03

Virtual try-on and sizing

Clothing visualisation, brand-specific sizing and fashion assistance.

04

Customer behaviour and staff assistance

Movement analysis, behaviour classification and staff support.
